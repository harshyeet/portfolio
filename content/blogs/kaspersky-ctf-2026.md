Okay, 2nd post on the website, and it's about my first ever CTF. Yeah so I just wrapped up my first CTF some time back — the Kaspersky CTF. As traumatizing as it was (more on that later), I genuinely had a lot of fun and I'm hooked on doing more of these. In fact, as I'm writing this, I just got done with another CTF (the TFCCTF), but I only solved one OSINT problem in it 'cause I've got my midterms coming up, which is also why this post is coming up late.

Anyways, quick outcome of the CTF — we finished #10 in India 🇮🇳, and #56 in APAC. (I had to calculate the worldwide ranking myself 'cause they didn't give us any, and we finished #124 out of 1622 teams.) Pretty good for my first CTF, tbf.

## The Trauma

Coming to the trauma — this was NOT what I expected in a CTF from a company like Kaspersky. There were a lot of "weird" instances, but here's my fav weird challenges:

**1. "Cold Dih" (AI)** — The challenge was to get the flag from 3 pigeon chatbots, who, btw, just wanted me to say the lyrics "I phone cold dih, a restaurant part dih..." and some other stuff. I texted them for like 2 hours before I gave up. I am never going to look at pigeons the same way again.

**2. SunHua Gate (Pwn)** — If the challenge port being 6767 didn't already make you raise your eyebrows, hold your horses — the program was ENTIRELY in Chinese. Me, kuro, and cracka.xss all lost our minds over SunHua, but well, we weren't able to get it to work :(
